# Frequently asked questions

**Sample questions — app-specific answers will be added before launch.**

## Where can I download an app?

Each app in the collection can include links to its App Store or Google Play listing. Available links appear beneath the app description.

## How do I join a beta?

If an app offers a beta, look for an **iOS beta** or **Android beta** link on its card.

## Where can I find the privacy policy?

The shared policy will be available on the [Privacy Policy page](../privacy/).
