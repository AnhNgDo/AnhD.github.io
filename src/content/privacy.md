# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 17 November 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications developed by **AnhD** that link to this policy ("our apps").

Our guiding principle is simple: **our apps work on your device without collecting or sending personal information to us.**

## 1. Information handled by our apps

Our apps do not require registration or an account. They do not collect personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers, or send that information to us.

We do not track how you use our apps, including the buttons you tap, the tasks you complete, or the time you spend using them. We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools in our apps.

## 2. Offline use and information on your device

Our apps are designed to work offline without an internet connection. Any settings, saved progress, or other information an app saves stays on your device and is not sent to us. We cannot access that information remotely.

Your device or operating system may include app data in backups, depending on your settings. Those backups are managed by your device or backup provider.

You can manage locally saved information through the app's available controls or your device settings. Removing an app may remove its local data, but copies in device backups may remain until you delete them separately.

## 3. Children's privacy

The same privacy practices apply to all users, including children. Our apps do not collect personal information from children or require children to provide personal information to use them. They do not include social media plugins or features for finding or contacting other users.

Parents and guardians can contact us with questions about an app's privacy practices using the email address below.

## 4. Sharing and third-party services

Because our apps do not collect personal information, we do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through them.

App stores, operating systems, and backup providers may process information independently under their own privacy policies. This policy covers our apps, rather than those services or the website hosting this policy.

## 5. Security

Keeping app information on your device limits the information we hold about you. The security of locally saved information also depends on your device and any backups you use. No device, storage system, or email service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our apps or privacy practices. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. If an app introduces practices outside the scope of this policy, we will update its privacy notice before those practices begin and obtain consent where required.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, please contact **AnhD** at [anh.ainsoft@gmail.com](mailto:anh.ainsoft@gmail.com).
