# Privacy Policy: Spelling Coach

**Effective Date:** June 8th, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Spelling Coach** mobile application (the "Application") created by **AnhD** ("Service Provider"). Our guiding principle is simple: **We do not collect, store, or share personal information.**

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to be used without any registration or account creation.

- **Personal Data:** The Application **does not collect** any personal information (such as name, email address, or phone number).
- **Usage Data:** We do not track how you use the app, what buttons you click, or how long you spend on specific tasks.
- **Offline Use:** The Application is designed to function entirely on your device without requiring an internet connection, ensuring your data stays on your device.

### 2. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because our Application is designed for children (specifically Year 2-4 students), we are committed to the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

- We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 (or any age).
- There are no social media plugins or "find a friend" features within the app.

### 3. Location Information

The Application **does not collect** precise, real-time, or approximate information about the location of your mobile device.

### 4. Third-Party Access and Advertising

Since no information is collected, no information can be shared with third parties.

- **No Ads:** Spelling Coach does not contain third-party advertisements.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics tools (like Google Analytics or Firebase) that track user behavior.

### 5. Security

We prioritize the safety of our users. By choosing a "Zero-Data" architecture, we have eliminated the risk of your personal data being compromised, accessed by unauthorized individuals, or leaked through a server breach. **Your data cannot be stolen if it is never collected.**

### 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. We encourage parents to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the Application after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.

### 7. Your Consent

By using the Application, you are consenting to this Privacy Policy.

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about our practices, please contact us [anh.ainsoft@gmail.com](mailto:anh.ainsoft@gmail.com).
