# Visual foundation

Approved direction: warm editorial app gallery. Code-first setup; app photography will be supplied by the owner.

- Paper: `#f7f5ef`; ink: `#252b26`; secondary text: `#5b645d`; accent: `#315b42`; rules: `#d9ddd3`.
- Display: self-hosted Newsreader 400. Body and controls: self-hosted DM Sans 400/500/700.
- Layout: maximum width 1160px; desktop margins 48px, mobile margins 20px.
- Collection: two columns, one below 700px; 4:3 featured images with 14px corners.
- Typography and open space provide hierarchy. Images lead the catalogue; prose pages retain the same navigation and reading rhythm.
- Links have visible keyboard focus; current navigation is underlined. Reduced motion removes transitions.
- Placeholder content is explicitly labeled and contains no fictitious store links or privacy promises.

Core tokens and responsive rules live in `src/styles/global.css`.
